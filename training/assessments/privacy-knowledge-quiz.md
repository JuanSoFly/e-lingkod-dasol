## Question Bank (Version A)

### Question 1: Basic Privacy Understanding
**Under the new E-Lingkod Dasol HRIS security system, as an employee you can:**

A) View all employee profiles to get contact information  
B) Access your own profile and employment data only  
C) See colleagues' leave balances for better coordination  
D) View any data you need for your work assignments

**Correct Answer**: B) Access your own profile and employment data only

**Explanation**: The new privacy protection system implements role-based access control. Employees can only access their own personal information to protect everyone's privacy and comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act.

**Learning Objective**: Understanding role-based access control

---

### Question 2: Incident Response
**If you accidentally see another employee's personal data in the system, you should:**

A) Ignore it and continue with your work  
B) Note the information for future reference  
C) Report it immediately to HR or IT support  
D) Share the information with your supervisor

**Correct Answer**: C) Report it immediately to HR or IT support

**Explanation**: Any unauthorized access to personal data, even if accidental, should be reported immediately. This helps protect the other person's privacy and allows the technical team to fix any system issues.

**Learning Objective**: Proper incident response procedures

---

### Question 3: Password Security
**Your HRIS login credentials should be:**

A) Shared with trusted colleagues for backup access  
B) Kept completely private and secure  
C) Written down in a secure location for reference  
D) Changed only when required by the system

**Correct Answer**: B) Kept completely private and secure

**Explanation**: Login credentials should never be shared with anyone, including trusted colleagues, supervisors, or IT staff. Real support requests will never ask for your existing password.

**Learning Objective**: Password security best practices

---

### Question 4: Session Security
**When you finish using the HRIS system, you should:**

A) Close the browser window  
B) Log out completely from the system  
C) Minimize the window for later use  
D) Leave it open for the next user

**Correct Answer**: B) Log out completely from the system

**Explanation**: Always use the logout button to end your session properly. This ensures your account is secure and prevents unauthorized access if someone else uses the computer.

**Learning Objective**: Secure session management

---

### Question 5: Audit and Monitoring
**The new audit logging system tracks:**

A) Only login and logout times  
B) All access to employee data and system usage  
C) Only administrative actions by HR staff  
D) Just security violations and incidents

**Correct Answer**: B) All access to employee data and system usage

**Explanation**: The comprehensive audit system logs all data access, system usage, and activities to ensure transparency, security, and compliance with privacy laws.

**Learning Objective**: Understanding audit and monitoring systems

---

### Question 6: Professional Boundaries
**If you need information about a colleague for work purposes, you should:**

A) Try to access their profile in the system  
B) Ask them directly for the information  
C) Contact the HR department for assistance  
D) Ask your supervisor to get the information

**Correct Answer**: C) Contact the HR department for assistance

**Explanation**: HR can provide necessary work-related information through proper channels while protecting everyone's privacy. This ensures compliance with data protection policies.

**Learning Objective**: Proper channels for accessing colleague information

---

### Question 7: Technical Security
**Strong passwords for the HRIS should include:**

A) Your name and employee number  
B) Common dictionary words  
C) A mix of letters, numbers, and symbols  
D) Your birthday or family names

**Correct Answer**: C) A mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

**Explanation**: Strong passwords must include at least 8 characters with uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Personal information makes passwords easily guessable.

**Learning Objective**: Password complexity requirements

---

### Question 8: Legal Rights
**Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:**

A) Access any employee's data for work purposes  
B) View and correct your own personal data  
C) Share employee data with external parties  
D) Ignore privacy policies if they're inconvenient

**Correct Answer**: B) View and correct your own personal data

**Explanation**: The Philippine Data Privacy Act grants you specific rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, and understand how your information is processed.

**Learning Objective**: Understanding data subject rights under Philippine law

---

### Question 9: Reporting Timeline
**Privacy violations should be reported:**

A) Only if they seem serious or harmful  
B) At the end of each work week  
C) Immediately when discovered  
D) During annual performance reviews

**Correct Answer**: C) Immediately when discovered

**Explanation**: Privacy violations should be reported immediately to prevent further exposure and allow for quick containment and investigation of the issue.

**Learning Objective**: Appropriate response timing for privacy incidents

---

### Question 10: System Purpose
**The main purpose of the new security features is to:**

A) Make the system harder to use  
B) Comply with legal requirements only  
C) Protect employee privacy and maintain trust  
D) Reduce server costs and improve performance

**Correct Answer**: C) Protect employee privacy and maintain trust

**Explanation**: The security enhancements are primarily designed to protect employee privacy rights, maintain trust in the system, and ensure compliance with data protection laws while maintaining functionality.

**Learning Objective**: Understanding the overall purpose of privacy protection measures

## Question Bank (Version B)

### Question 1: Data Classification
**Which of the following is considered sensitive personal information that requires special protection:**

A) Your employee number and department  
B) Your government ID numbers and health records  
C) Your work email address and phone extension  
D) Your job title and supervisor's name

**Correct Answer**: B) Your government ID numbers and health records

**Explanation**: Sensitive personal information includes government IDs, health records, financial information, and family details that could cause harm if misused.

---

### Question 2: System Access Boundaries
**The privacy protection system prevents employees from accessing:**

A) Their own leave balance history  
B) Their personal performance evaluations  
C) All employee directory listings  
D) Their own document request status

**Correct Answer**: C) All employee directory listings

**Explanation**: Employees cannot access comprehensive employee directories or lists that contain other employees' personal information. This protects privacy while maintaining necessary self-service functionality.

---

### Question 3: Security Alerts
**If you receive a security alert about unusual activity on your account, you should:**

A) Ignore it if you remember your recent activity  
B) Contact IT support to investigate immediately  
C) Change your password only if you think it's serious  
D) Wait to see if you get more alerts

**Correct Answer**: B) Contact IT support to investigate immediately

**Explanation**: All security alerts should be taken seriously and investigated promptly, even if you think you know what caused them. This helps ensure your account remains secure.

---

### Question 4: Social Engineering
**Someone calls claiming to be from IT and asks for your password to "fix your account." You should:**

A) Give them your password since they're from IT  
B) Refuse to provide your password and report the call  
C) Ask them to prove they're from IT first  
D) Hang up and call back using the main IT number

**Correct Answer**: B) Refuse to provide your password and report the call

**Explanation**: Legitimate IT support will never ask for your existing password. They can reset passwords without knowing the current one. This is likely a social engineering attempt.

---

### Question 5: Data Retention
**How long does the system keep audit logs of data access:**

A) 30 days for basic activities  
B) 1 year for all activities  
C) According to legal and compliance requirements  
D) Only while you're employed

**Correct Answer**: C) According to legal and compliance requirements

**Explanation**: Audit logs are retained according to legal and regulatory requirements, which may vary by type of activity and compliance obligations.

---

### Question 6: Privacy Violation Detection
**The system automatically detects privacy violations by:**

A) Only monitoring failed login attempts  
B) Tracking all data access patterns in real-time  
C) Checking for viruses and malware  
D) Recording only administrative activities

**Correct Answer**: B) Tracking all data access patterns in real-time

**Explanation**: The system monitors all data access in real-time to detect unauthorized attempts and privacy violations immediately, allowing for quick response.

---

### Question 7: Mobile Access
**When accessing the HRIS from a mobile device, you should:**

A) Save your password for convenience  
B) Use any available Wi-Fi connection  
C) Use secure networks and log out completely  
D) Keep the app running in the background

**Correct Answer**: C) Use secure networks and log out completely

**Explanation**: Mobile access requires the same security precautions as desktop access: secure networks, complete logout, and never saving passwords on devices.

---

### Question 8: Data Correction Rights
**If you find incorrect information in your personnel record, you should:**

A) Wait for HR to fix it during annual reviews  
B) Use the system's update features or contact HR  
C) Ignore minor errors that don't affect your pay  
D) Ask a colleague to help you change it

**Correct Answer**: B) Use the system's update features or contact HR

**Explanation**: You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data. Use the system's update features for simple changes or contact HR for complex corrections requiring documentation.

---

### Question 9: System Performance
**If the system seems slower after the security updates, this is:**

A) A sign that something is wrong with your computer  
B) Normal due to additional security checks  
C) An indication that you should call IT immediately  
D) A reason to use fewer security features

**Correct Answer**: B) Normal due to additional security checks

**Explanation**: Some slight performance impact is normal due to additional security checks that protect your data. Report only significant slowdowns to IT support.

---

### Question 10: Training Requirements
**Completion of privacy training is:**

A) Recommended but not required  
B) Required only for new employees  
C) Required for all system users  
D) Only necessary for HR staff

**Correct Answer**: C) Required for all system users

**Explanation**: All users must complete privacy training to ensure they understand their responsibilities and can use the system safely and legally.

## Question Bank (Version C - Advanced Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: Workplace Situation
**Your supervisor asks you to look up a colleague's contact information for a work project. The system won't let you access it. What should you do?**

A) Try different search terms to find the information  
B) Ask the colleague directly or have supervisor contact HR  
C) Use your supervisor's login to access the information  
D) Report the system as broken to IT support

**Correct Answer**: B) Ask the colleague directly or have supervisor contact HR

**Explanation**: This is the privacy protection working correctly. Contact the person directly or have your supervisor work through HR for legitimate business needs.

---

### Scenario 2: Technical Issue
**You're working on a performance review and the system suddenly shows you someone else's evaluation data. What's your immediate response?**

A) Quickly review the information since it's already displayed  
B) Take a screenshot to report the technical error  
C) Close the screen immediately and report to IT/HR  
D) Try to navigate back to your own information

**Correct Answer**: C) Close the screen immediately and report to IT/HR

**Explanation**: This is a serious privacy violation that needs immediate reporting. Don't review, screenshot, or try to navigate - just close and report.

---

### Scenario 3: Social Engineering
**A colleague mentions they forgot their password and asks if they can use your computer to quickly check their leave balance. How do you respond?**

A) Let them use your login since it's just for leave information  
B) Politely decline and direct them to IT for password reset  
C) Log them in but watch to make sure they only check leave  
D) Give them your password so they can log in themselves

**Correct Answer**: B) Politely decline and direct them to IT for password reset

**Explanation**: Never share your login credentials or computer access with anyone. Direct them to proper IT support for legitimate assistance.
