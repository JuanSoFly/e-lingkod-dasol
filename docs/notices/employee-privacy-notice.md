## Why We Process Your Data (Legal Basis)

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

### **1. Employment Contract Performance**
Most of your data is processed because it's necessary to fulfill our employment relationship with you:
- Managing your employment record and 201 file
- Processing your salary and benefits
- Managing your leave and attendance
- Providing workplace facilities and services
- Managing your performance and career development

### **2. Legal Obligations**
We are required by Philippine law to process certain data:
- **Civil Service Commission**: Monthly reports (accession, separation, DIBAR), annual IGHR
- **Bureau of Internal Revenue**: Tax reporting and withholding
- **Social Security System**: Contribution reporting and member records
- **GSIS**: Insurance premium processing and claims
- **PhilHealth**: Health insurance enrollment and contributions
- **Pag-IBIG**: Housing fund contributions and loan processing

### **3. Legitimate Interests (with appropriate safeguards)**
For specific purposes where we have legitimate interests:
- Workplace security and safety
- Performance management and improvement
- Training and development programs
- Internal communications and coordination
- Legal defense and compliance monitoring

**Safeguards**: We ensure that your privacy rights are protected and that processing is proportionate to our legitimate needs.

### **4. Your Consent (limited situations)**
In specific cases, we may ask for your explicit consent:
- Optional training programs or professional development
- Participation in research or surveys
- Use of your photo or information for promotional materials
- Additional data processing beyond employment requirements

**You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting your employment.**

## Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### **1. Right of Access**
You have the right to:
- Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data
- Obtain access to your personal data
- Receive information about how we process your data
- Get copies of your personal data in an intelligible format

**How to Exercise**: Submit a written request to the HR Department with valid identification
**Response Time**: Maximum 15 working days
**Cost**: First request per year is free; subsequent requests may incur reasonable costs

### **2. Right to Rectification (Correction)**
You have the right to:
- Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- Request completion of incomplete personal data
- Update your contact information and other changeable details
- Provide additional information where relevant

**How to Exercise**: 
- Use the self-service features in the HRIS for basic updates
- Submit written request to HR for complex corrections
- Provide supporting documentation where necessary
**Response Time**: Immediate for system updates, maximum 5 working days for manual corrections

### **3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**
You have the right to request deletion of your personal data when:
- The data is no longer necessary for the original purpose
- You withdraw consent (where consent was the legal basis)
- The data has been processed unlawfully
- Erasure is required for legal compliance

**Important Limitations in Employment Context**:
- Core employment data must be retained during employment
- Legal retention requirements override erasure requests
- Some data may be needed for legal defense or claims

**How to Exercise**: Submit written request with justification to HR Department
**Response Time**: 30 working days with explanation of decision

### **4. Right to Data Portability**
You have the right to:
- Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format
- Transmit your data to another organization
- Request direct transmission where technically feasible

**Scope**: Data you provided and system-generated employment data
**Format**: JSON, CSV, or PDF format
**How to Exercise**: Request through HR Department or self-service portal
**Response Time**: 30 working days maximum

### **5. Right to Object**
You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests when:
- You have compelling legitimate grounds relating to your particular situation
- The processing affects your fundamental rights and freedoms

**Process**: Submit written objection with reasoning to HR Department
**Assessment**: We will conduct a balancing test between your rights and our legitimate interests
**Response Time**: 30 working days with decision and reasoning

### **6. Right to Restrict Processing**
You can request restriction of processing when:
- You contest the accuracy of personal data (during verification period)
- Processing is unlawful but you prefer restriction over erasure
- We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims
- You have objected to processing (during assessment period)

**How to Exercise**: Submit written request to HR Department
**Effect**: We will store but not further process the restricted data
**Response Time**: 5 working days for implementation

## How We Protect Your Data

- **Role-Based Permissions**: Access limited to job requirements
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Additional security for administrative access
- **Strong Password Requirements**: Minimum 8 characters with complexity rules
- **Session Management**: Automatic timeout and secure session handling
- **Regular Access Reviews**: Quarterly review and certification of user access

#### **Data Encryption**
- **Transmission Security**: All data transmitted using HTTPS encryption
- **Database Encryption**: Sensitive data encrypted in database storage
- **Backup Encryption**: All backups encrypted and securely stored
- **File Encryption**: Documents and files encrypted at rest

#### **Network Security**
- **Firewall Protection**: Network perimeter security
- **Intrusion Detection**: Monitoring for unauthorized access attempts
- **Secure Networks**: VPN requirements for remote access
- **Regular Updates**: Security patches and system updates

#### **Monitoring and Logging**
- **Activity Logging**: All data access logged with user identification
- **Privacy Violation Detection**: Automated alerts for suspicious activity
- **Audit Trails**: Complete record of all system activities
- **Log Protection**: Audit logs secured and tamper-proof

#### **Personnel Security**
- **Background Checks**: Verification of personnel with system access

- **Role-Specific Training**: Additional training based on job responsibilities

#### **Physical Security**
- **Facility Security**: Restricted access to server rooms and data storage areas
- **Workstation Security**: Automatic screen locks and secure document disposal
- **Mobile Device Management**: Security requirements for mobile access
- **Visitor Controls**: Escort requirements for non-employees

#### **Vendor Management**
- **Security Assessments**: Evaluation of vendor security practices
- **Contractual Requirements**: Security obligations in all service contracts
- **Regular Audits**: Periodic assessment of vendor compliance
- **Incident Reporting**: Immediate notification of vendor security incidents

### **Backup and Recovery**
- **Regular Backups**: Daily automated backups of all system data
- **Secure Storage**: Encrypted backup storage in secure facilities
- **Recovery Testing**: Regular testing of backup and recovery procedures
- **Business Continuity**: Disaster recovery plans for system restoration

## Data Retention and Deletion

### **Retention Principles**
We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law.

### **Retention Periods**

#### **During Active Employment**
- **All Employment Data**: Retained throughout employment period
- **Regular Updates**: Annual review and verification of data accuracy
- **Continuous Processing**: Ongoing processing for employment administration

#### **After Employment Separation**

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---------------|------------------|-------------|
| **General Employment Records** | 5 years | Civil Service Commission requirements |
| **Payroll and Tax Records** | 10 years | Bureau of Internal Revenue requirements |
| **GSIS/Insurance Records** | 30 years | GSIS requirements for lifetime benefits |
| **PhilHealth Records** | 10 years | PhilHealth regulations |
| **Performance Records** | 5 years | Reference and legal protection |
| **Disciplinary Records** | 5 years or duration of legal proceedings | Legal compliance and defense |
| **Training Certificates** | Permanent | Career reference and verification |
| **Medical Information** | 10 years | Potential liability protection |

#### **System and Audit Data**

| Data Type | Retention Period | Purpose |
|-----------|------------------|---------|
| **Security Logs** | 3 years | Security monitoring and incident investigation |
| **Access Logs** | 2 years | Compliance monitoring |
| **System Backups** | 1 year rolling | System recovery and business continuity |
| **Privacy Violation Logs** | 5 years | Compliance monitoring and investigation |

### **Secure Deletion**
When retention periods expire:
1. **Automated Archival**: System automatically archives expired data
2. **Secure Deletion**: Data permanently deleted using secure deletion methods
3. **Verification**: Deletion verified and documented
4. **Exception Handling**: Legal holds override automatic deletion
5. **Audit Trail**: All deletion activities logged for compliance

## International Data Transfers

### **General Policy**
Your personal data is processed and stored exclusively within the Philippines. We do not transfer your data to other countries unless specifically required and authorized.

### **Prohibited Transfers**
- **Commercial Purposes**: No data sales or marketing transfers to foreign entities
- **Convenience Transfers**: No transfers for administrative convenience
- **Cloud Storage**: No storage on foreign servers or cloud services
- **Remote Access**: No routine access from foreign locations

### **Limited Exceptions**
We may transfer data internationally only in these specific circumstances:

#### **Legal Requirements**
- Court orders from Philippine courts requiring international cooperation
- Treaty obligations requiring data sharing with foreign governments
- Official diplomatic or consular services for employees abroad

#### **Employee-Initiated Transfers**
- Employee relocates internationally and requests data transfer
- Employee explicitly consents to specific international transfer
- Employee education or training programs abroad

#### **Emergency Situations**
- Medical emergencies requiring international medical treatment
- Evacuation or emergency assistance for employees abroad
- Urgent legal matters requiring international legal assistance

### **Transfer Safeguards**
When international transfers are authorized:
1. **Adequacy Assessment**: Evaluation of destination country privacy laws
2. **Appropriate Safeguards**: Binding agreements or standard contractual clauses
3. **Employee Notification**: Advance notice to affected employees
4. **Limited Scope**: Minimum necessary data only
5. **Documentation**: Complete record of transfer basis and safeguards
6. **Monitoring**: Ongoing monitoring of data protection in destination

## Automated Decision-Making

### **Limited Use of Automated Decisions**
The HRIS system includes some automated processing, but significant decisions affecting you are not made solely by automated means.

### **Automated Processes in Use**

#### **Administrative Automation**
- **Leave Balance Calculations**: Automatic calculation of leave credits and balances
- **Payroll Processing**: Automated salary and deduction calculations
- **Report Generation**: Automatic generation of standard reports
- **System Notifications**: Automated alerts and reminders

#### **Performance Support**
- **IPCR Calculations**: Automated rating calculations based on input data
- **Training Recommendations**: System suggestions based on performance gaps

### **Human Oversight Required**
The following decisions always require human review and cannot be made solely by automated systems:
- **Performance Ratings**: Final performance evaluations
- **Disciplinary Actions**: All disciplinary decisions
- **Promotions and Assignments**: Career advancement decisions
- **Benefit Approvals**: Complex benefit determinations
- **Leave Approvals**: Extended or complex leave requests

### **Your Rights Regarding Automated Decisions**
- **Right to Human Review**: Request human review of any automated decision
- **Right to Contest**: Challenge automated decisions affecting you
- **Right to Explanation**: Understand the logic behind automated decisions
- **Right to Correction**: Request correction of automated calculations
