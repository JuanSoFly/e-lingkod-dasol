## Executive Summary

This Compliance Monitoring Framework establishes systematic procedures for ongoing assessment, monitoring, and improvement of privacy and data protection compliance within the E-Lingkod Dasol HRIS system. The framework ensures continuous compliance with Philippine Data Privacy Act requirements and international best practices.

**Framework Scope**: All aspects of data protection and privacy compliance  
**Monitoring Frequency**: Continuous monitoring with scheduled reviews  
**Compliance Target**: 100% adherence to regulatory requirements  
**Current Status**: Framework implementation 95% complete

## Monitoring Framework Overview

### 1. Framework Objectives

#### Primary Objectives
- **Continuous Compliance**: Maintain 100% compliance with DPA requirements
- **Risk Management**: Identify and mitigate privacy risks proactively
- **Performance Optimization**: Continuously improve compliance processes
- **Stakeholder Assurance**: Provide confidence to employees and management

#### Secondary Objectives
- **Regulatory Readiness**: Prepare for regulatory inspections and audits
- **Incident Prevention**: Detect and prevent privacy violations

#### A. Technical Monitoring

#### B. Organizational Monitoring
- **Policy Compliance**: Assessment of policy adherence
- **Training Effectiveness**: Evaluation of staff competency

- **Legal Updates**: Tracking of regulatory changes

## Monitoring Schedule and Procedures

### Daily Monitoring Activities

#### Automated System Monitoring
**Time**: 24/7 Continuous  
**Responsible**: IT Security Manager  
**Tools**: Security monitoring dashboard, automated alerts

**Daily Checks**:
- ✅ System access logs review
- ✅ Failed authentication attempts
- ✅ Unusual data access patterns
- ✅ System performance metrics
- ✅ Security alert triage

**Success Criteria**:
- Zero unauthorized access attempts
- All access patterns within normal parameters
- System performance within acceptable thresholds
- Security alerts resolved within 2 hours

#### Privacy Incident Monitoring
**Time**: Business hours (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM)  
**Responsible**: Data Protection Officer  
**Tools**: Incident management system, audit logs

**Daily Checks**:
- ✅ Review incident reports
- ✅ Assess potential privacy violations
- ✅ Verify incident response procedures
- ✅ Update incident tracking dashboard

**Success Criteria**:
- All incidents documented within 1 hour
- Risk assessment completed within 4 hours
- Appropriate response initiated within 8 hours
- Incident resolution tracked to completion
