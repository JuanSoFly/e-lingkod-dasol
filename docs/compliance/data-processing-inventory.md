## Executive Summary

This Data Processing Inventory documents all personal data processing activities conducted by the Municipality of Dasol through the E-Lingkod Dasol Human Resource Information System (HRIS). This inventory ensures compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (Republic Act No. 10173) and supports the organization's accountability obligations.

## Personal Data Categories Processed

### 1. Employee Personal Data

#### Basic Information
- **Data Elements**: Full name, employee number, date of birth, gender, civil status
- **Source**: Employee registration, official records
- **Purpose**: Personnel identification and administration
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract performance
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 5 years

#### Contact Information
- **Data Elements**: Home address, contact numbers, email addresses
- **Source**: Employee self-declaration, HR records
- **Purpose**: Communication, emergency contact, official correspondence
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract performance
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 3 years

#### Government Identification
- **Data Elements**: SSS number, GSIS number, PhilHealth number, Pag-IBIG number, TIN
- **Source**: Employee submission, government agencies
- **Purpose**: Government benefits administration, tax compliance
- **Legal Basis**: Legal obligation (government reporting requirements)
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 10 years (tax records)

#### Employment Data
- **Data Elements**: Position title, department, salary grade, employment status, appointment date
- **Source**: HR appointments, Civil Service Commission
- **Purpose**: Personnel management, payroll processing, CSC reporting
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract + legal obligation
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 5 years

### 2. Sensitive Personal Information

#### Financial Information
- **Data Elements**: Salary amounts, allowances, deductions, bank account details
- **Source**: Payroll system, employee declarations
- **Purpose**: Payroll processing, financial reporting
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract performance
- **Security Measures**: Encrypted storage, restricted access
- **Retention**: 10 years (BIR requirements)

#### Performance Data
- **Data Elements**: IPCR ratings, performance evaluations, disciplinary records
- **Source**: Performance management system, supervisors
- **Purpose**: Performance management, career development, promotions
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract + legitimate interest
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 5 years

#### Health Information (Limited)
- **Data Elements**: Medical certificate data (leave applications only)
- **Source**: Employee-submitted medical certificates
- **Purpose**: Leave application processing
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract performance
- **Special Protection**: Medical data handled separately, restricted access
- **Retention**: 5 years

#### Educational Background
- **Data Elements**: Degrees, certifications, training records, civil service eligibility
- **Source**: Employee submissions, training providers, CSC
- **Purpose**: Qualification verification, training management, career development
- **Legal Basis**: Employment contract + legal obligation (CSC requirements)
- **Retention**: Duration of employment + 5 years

## Data Processing Purposes

### 1. Employment Administration
- **Primary Purpose**: Personnel management and payroll processing
- **Activities**: 
  - Employee record maintenance
  - Payroll calculation and distribution
  - Benefits administration
  - Performance management
- **Data Used**: All employee personal data categories
- **Frequency**: Ongoing during employment

### 2. Legal Compliance
- **Primary Purpose**: Meeting government reporting and regulatory requirements
- **Activities**:
  - Civil Service Commission monthly reports (Accession, Separation, DIBAR)
  - Bureau of Internal Revenue tax reporting
  - GSIS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG reporting
  - Annual IGHR submission to CSC
- **Data Used**: Employment data, financial information, government IDs
- **Frequency**: Monthly and annual reporting cycles

### 3. Benefits Management
- **Primary Purpose**: Government and private benefits administration
- **Activities**:
  - GSIS enrollment and premium processing
  - PhilHealth registration and claims
  - Pag-IBIG contributions and loans
  - Leave credit management
- **Data Used**: Government IDs, employment data, health information (limited)
- **Frequency**: Ongoing, monthly contributions

### 4. Performance Management
- **Primary Purpose**: Employee development and evaluation
- **Activities**:
  - IPCR (Individual Performance Commitment and Review) processing
  - Performance rating compilation
  - Training needs assessment
  - Career progression planning
- **Data Used**: Performance data, educational background, training records
- **Frequency**: Semi-annual performance cycles

### 5. Document Management
- **Primary Purpose**: Official document generation and record keeping
- **Activities**:
  - Service record maintenance
  - Certificate generation (employment, service)
  - 201 file management
  - Document version control
- **Data Used**: All employee data as relevant to requested documents
- **Frequency**: On-demand document requests

## Legal Basis for Processing

### 1. Contract Performance
- **Basis**: Employment contracts between Municipality of Dasol and employees
- **Application**: All data processing necessary for employment relationship
- **Data Categories**: Basic information, contact details, employment data
- **Documentation**: Individual employment contracts on file

### 2. Legal Obligation
- **Basis**: Philippine laws requiring government employer compliance
- **Specific Laws**:
  - Civil Service Act (CSC reporting)
  - National Internal Revenue Code (tax reporting)
  - Social Security Act (SSS contributions)
  - GSIS Act (government insurance)
  - National Health Insurance Act (PhilHealth)
- **Data Categories**: Government IDs, financial information, employment status
- **Documentation**: Regulatory requirements documented in compliance manual

### 3. Legitimate Interest (with Safeguards)
- **Basis**: Municipality's legitimate interest in effective HR administration
- **Application**: Performance management, training coordination, workplace safety
- **Safeguards**: Role-based access control, audit logging, data minimization
- **Data Categories**: Performance data, training records, contact information
- **Balancing Test**: Employee privacy rights balanced against administrative efficiency

### 4. Consent (Limited Use)
- **Basis**: Explicit employee consent for specific uses
- **Application**: Optional training programs, professional development opportunities
- **Documentation**: Written consent forms maintained
- **Withdrawal**: Employees may withdraw consent without penalty

## Data Sources and Collection

### 1. Direct Collection from Employees
- **Method**: Employee registration forms, self-service portal updates
- **Data Types**: Personal information, contact details, educational background
- **Consent**: Informed consent provided through employment agreement
- **Frequency**: Initial registration + ongoing updates

### 2. Government Agencies
- **Sources**: Civil Service Commission, Bureau of Internal Revenue, GSIS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG
- **Data Types**: Official employment records, contribution histories, eligibility data
- **Legal Basis**: Government-to-government data sharing for regulatory compliance
- **Frequency**: Periodic updates as required by law

### 3. Internal Generation
- **Sources**: System-generated logs, performance evaluations, payroll calculations
- **Data Types**: Audit trails, performance ratings, financial computations
- **Purpose**: Administrative efficiency and compliance monitoring
- **Frequency**: Continuous (system logs), periodic (evaluations)

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 1. Internal Sharing Within Municipality
- **Recipients**: 
  - HR Department (full access for administration)
  - Immediate supervisors (performance-related data only)
  - Finance Department (payroll-related data only)
  - IT Support (technical maintenance, encrypted data only)
- **Purpose**: Legitimate business functions
- **Safeguards**: Role-based access control, audit logging, confidentiality agreements

### 2. External Sharing with Government Agencies
- **Recipients**:
  - Civil Service Commission (employment data for compliance reporting)
  - Bureau of Internal Revenue (financial data for tax compliance)
  - GSIS (member data for insurance administration)
  - PhilHealth (health insurance enrollment data)
  - Pag-IBIG (housing fund contribution data)
- **Legal Basis**: Legal obligation under Philippine law
- **Data Protection**: Secure transmission, minimal necessary data only
- **Frequency**: As required by law (monthly/annual cycles)

### 3. Authorized Third Parties
- **Recipients**: 
  - Payroll service providers (if outsourced)
  - IT system vendors (for technical support only)
  - Legal counsel (for legal advice on employment matters)
- **Purpose**: Service delivery and professional advice
- **Safeguards**: Data processing agreements, confidentiality clauses, security requirements
- **Oversight**: Regular audits of third-party compliance

### 4. Prohibited Disclosures
- **Never Shared With**:
  - Commercial entities for marketing purposes
  - Other employees (except as authorized)
  - Media or public disclosure
  - Foreign governments or agencies
- **Exception**: Legal orders from Philippine courts or authorized agencies only

## Data Retention Periods

### 1. Active Employment Records
- **Retention**: Duration of employment
- **Access**: Full access by authorized personnel
- **Review**: Annual accuracy review required
- **Updates**: Employee-initiated or HR-initiated as needed

### 2. Post-Employment Records
- **General Rule**: 5 years after separation
- **Tax Records**: 10 years (BIR requirement)
- **GSIS/PhilHealth**: 30 years (insurance requirements)
- **Disciplinary Records**: 5 years or duration of any legal proceedings
- **Performance Records**: 5 years for reference purposes

### 3. Audit Logs and System Records
- **Security Logs**: 3 years minimum
- **Access Logs**: 2 years
- **System Backups**: 1 year rolling retention
- **Privacy Violation Logs**: 5 years for compliance monitoring

### 4. Special Categories
- **Medical Information**: 10 years (potential liability protection)
- **Investigation Records**: Duration of any legal proceedings + 5 years
- **Training Certificates**: Lifetime (career reference)
- **Civil Service Eligibility**: Permanent record

## Data Security Measures

### 1. Technical Safeguards
- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication
- **Encryption**: Data in transit (HTTPS), sensitive data at rest
- **Network Security**: Firewall protection, intrusion detection
- **Backup Systems**: Regular encrypted backups, secure storage
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive activity monitoring and logging

### 2. Organizational Safeguards
- **Personnel Security**: Background checks, confidentiality agreements
- **Training Programs**: Annual data privacy training for all staff
- **Access Management**: Regular review of user permissions and access rights
- **Incident Response**: Documented procedures for data breaches
- **Vendor Management**: Security requirements in all service contracts

### 3. Physical Safeguards
- **Facility Security**: Restricted access to server rooms and data storage
- **Workstation Security**: Automatic screen locks, secure disposal of documents
- **Mobile Device Management**: Encryption requirements, remote wipe capabilities
- **Document Handling**: Secure storage and disposal of physical records

## Data Subject Rights Implementation

### 1. Right of Access
- **Process**: Employee request through HR or self-service portal
- **Timeline**: 15 working days maximum
- **Format**: Digital copy provided unless otherwise requested
- **Verification**: Employee ID verification required

### 2. Right to Rectification
- **Process**: Employee-initiated correction requests through system or HR
- **Timeline**: Immediate for system updates, 5 working days for manual corrections
- **Notification**: Affected third parties notified of corrections where applicable
- **Documentation**: All corrections logged for audit purposes

### 3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Limitations**: Employment context limits erasure rights
- **Available When**: Data no longer necessary for original purpose, consent withdrawn
- **Process**: Formal written request with justification required
- **Legal Override**: Retention required by law takes precedence

### 4. Right to Data Portability
- **Format**: Structured, commonly used electronic format (JSON, CSV)
- **Scope**: Data provided by employee and system-generated employment data
- **Process**: Request through HR with verification
- **Timeline**: 30 working days maximum

### 5. Right to Object
- **Grounds**: Legitimate grounds relating to particular situation
- **Process**: Written objection with reasoning
- **Assessment**: Balancing test between employee rights and legitimate interests
- **Response**: 30 working days with decision and reasoning

## Cross-Border Data Transfers

### 1. General Prohibition
- **Policy**: Employee data remains within Philippine jurisdiction
- **Exceptions**: Only as required by law or with explicit consent
- **Safeguards**: Adequacy decisions or appropriate safeguards required

### 2. Permitted Transfers
- **Cloud Services**: Philippine-based data centers only
- **Government Reporting**: To authorized Philippine government agencies only
- **Legal Proceedings**: As required by Philippine courts
- **Employee Relocation**: With consent and adequate protection

### 3. Prohibited Transfers
- **Commercial Purposes**: No data sales or marketing transfers
- **Foreign Governments**: No sharing with foreign authorities
- **Outsourcing**: No offshore processing without adequate safeguards
- **Personal Use**: No personal access from foreign locations
