## Welcome to the Secure E-Lingkod Dasol HRIS

- **Faster Performance**: Your data loads quickly because it's filtered and optimized
- **Relevant Menus**: You'll only see options you can actually use
- **Clear Messages**: Error messages explain what you can and cannot do

## 🚫 What You Cannot Access

To protect everyone's privacy and comply with data protection laws, the following are restricted:

### Other Employees' Information
- ❌ Colleagues' personal information and contact details
- ❌ Other employees' leave records and balances
- ❌ Colleagues' salary and compensation information
- ❌ Other employees' document requests and certificates
- ❌ Performance evaluations of other staff members

### Administrative Functions
- ❌ System-wide reports and analytics
- ❌ All employee directories and lists
- ❌ Payroll and compensation management
- ❌ System configuration and user management
- ❌ Audit logs and security reports

### Why These Restrictions Exist
These limitations are in place to:
- **Protect Privacy**: Ensure everyone's personal data remains confidential
- **Follow the Law**: Comply with Philippine Data Privacy Act requirements
- **Maintain Trust**: Build confidence in the system's security
- **Prevent Misuse**: Avoid unauthorized access to sensitive information

## 🔐 How to Use the System Securely

### Logging In Safely

#### Strong Password Requirements
Your password must include:
- **Minimum 8 characters** with no maximum limit
- **At least one uppercase letter** (A-Z)
- **At least one lowercase letter** (a-z)  
- **At least one number** (0-9)
- **At least one special character** (@, $, !, %, *, ?, &)

#### Good Password Examples
- `MyWork2025!` ✅
- `Dasol#Employee99` ✅
- `SecurePass@2025` ✅

#### Poor Password Examples  
- `password123` ❌ (too simple)
- `dasol2025` ❌ (no special characters)
- `PASSWORD` ❌ (no numbers or lowercase)

#### Login Security Best Practices
1. **Never share your password** with anyone, including supervisors
2. **Don't write passwords down** or store them in unsecured locations
3. **Change your password** if you suspect it has been compromised
4. **Use a unique password** not used for other accounts
5. **Log out completely** when finished using the system

### Secure Session Management

#### During Your Session
- **Verify you're accessing your own data** before proceeding
- **Don't leave your computer unattended** while logged in
- **Don't share your screen** when viewing personal information
- **Use secure networks** when accessing the system remotely
- **Report any unusual activity** immediately

#### Ending Your Session
1. **Click "Logout"** in the top navigation menu
2. **Close the browser tab** completely
3. **Clear browser cache** if using a shared computer
4. **Lock your workstation** when stepping away

## 🛡️ Your Privacy Rights Under Philippine Law

### Right to Access Your Data
You have the right to:
- **View all personal data** we have about you in the system
- **Request copies** of your personnel records (201 files)
- **Understand how your data** is being processed and used
- **Access your data** at any reasonable time during business hours

**How to Exercise**: Use the self-service features in the system or contact the HR department for comprehensive records.

### Right to Correct Your Information
You have the right to:
- **Update your contact information** through the system
- **Correct any inaccurate personal data** in your profile
- **Request HR review** for complex corrections requiring documentation
- **Ensure your records are accurate** and up-to-date

**How to Exercise**: Use the "Edit Profile" function for basic updates, or submit a formal request to HR for complex changes.

### Right to Information
You have the right to:
- **Understand what data we collect** about you
- **Know why we process** your personal information
- **Learn how long we keep** your employment records
- **Receive privacy notices** about data processing activities

**How to Exercise**: Review the Employee Privacy Notice or contact the HR department for detailed information.

### Right to Lodge Complaints
You have the right to:
- **File complaints** about privacy violations
- **Report concerns** to the HR department
- **Escalate issues** to the National Privacy Commission if needed
- **Receive timely responses** to your privacy concerns

**How to Exercise**: Contact Mr. Bryan Navalta Balaoing (Data Protection Officer) or file a formal complaint with supporting documentation.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General System Questions

**Q: Why can't I see other employees' information anymore?**  
**A**: This is a security improvement to protect everyone's privacy and comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act. You can only access information you need for your work, which helps maintain confidentiality and trust.

**Q: How do I know my data is secure?**  
**A**: All access to your data is logged, encrypted, and monitored. Only authorized personnel can view your information for legitimate business purposes. The system includes multiple layers of security protection.

**Q: What if I need information about a colleague for work purposes?**  
**A**: Contact the HR department. They can provide necessary information through proper channels while protecting everyone's privacy rights.

**Q: Can I request a copy of my personnel records?**  
**A**: Yes, you have the legal right to request copies of your 201 file and other personnel records. Contact HR to make this request with proper identification.

### Security and Privacy Questions

**Q: How often should I change my password?**  
**A**: Change your password immediately if you suspect it's been compromised. Otherwise, follow your organization's password policy or change it every 6 months for good security practice.

**Q: What happens if I forget my password?**  
**A**: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it securely. You'll receive instructions via your registered email address.

**Q: Can I access the system from home?**  
**A**: If remote access is permitted by your organization, ensure you're using a secure network and follow all security guidelines. Never access the system from public computers or unsecured networks.

**Q: What if I accidentally access unauthorized information?**  
**A**: Report it immediately to IT support or HR. Don't attempt to access it again, and don't share the information with anyone.

### Troubleshooting Questions

**Q: The system is running slowly. Is this a security issue?**  
**A**: Slow performance can have various causes. If you notice unusual slowness combined with other strange behavior, report it to IT support for investigation.

**Q: I'm getting error messages. What should I do?**  
**A**: Note the exact error message and when it occurs. Contact IT support with this information so they can help resolve the issue.

**Q: My session keeps timing out. Is this normal?**  
**A**: Yes, sessions timeout after 2 hours of inactivity for security reasons. Save your work frequently and log back in when needed.

## 📋 Best Practices for Data Security

### Personal Responsibility

#### Protecting Your Access
- **Keep login credentials secure** and never share them
- **Use strong, unique passwords** that meet system requirements
- **Report security concerns immediately** to appropriate personnel
- **Don't attempt to access unauthorized information** or functions
- **Follow organizational data protection policies** at all times

#### When Using the System
- **Verify you're accessing your own data** before proceeding with any actions
- **Log out completely** when finished, especially on shared computers
- **Don't share your screen** when viewing personal or sensitive information
- **Use secure networks** when accessing the system remotely
- **Keep your contact information updated** for security notifications

#### Maintaining Awareness
- **Read privacy notices** and policy updates when they're distributed
- **Attend security awareness training** when scheduled
- **Ask questions** if you're unsure about data handling procedures
- **Report privacy concerns promptly** instead of ignoring them
- **Stay informed** about your rights and responsibilities

### Working with Personal Data

#### Your Own Data
- **Keep your profile information current** and accurate
- **Review your data regularly** for any discrepancies
- **Understand what data is collected** and why it's needed
- **Exercise your privacy rights** when appropriate
- **Secure your personal documents** and certificates

#### Respecting Others' Privacy
- **Don't attempt to access** colleagues' personal information
- **Respect confidentiality** in all work interactions
- **Report privacy violations** if you become aware of them
- **Support the privacy culture** in your workplace
- **Lead by example** in following security procedures

### Emergency Procedures

#### If You Suspect a Security Breach
1. **Stop using the system** immediately
2. **Don't attempt to investigate** on your own
3. **Report to IT support** or HR immediately
4. **Document what you observed** for the report
5. **Follow up** to ensure the issue is being addressed

#### If Your Account is Compromised
1. **Change your password** immediately if possible
2. **Contact IT support** for assistance
3. **Review your recent activity** for unauthorized actions
4. **Report any suspicious activity** you discover
5. **Monitor your account** closely after resolution
