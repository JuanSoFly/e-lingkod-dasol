**Audience**: All Users  
**Difficulty**: Beginner  
**Estimated Reading Time**: 10 minutes

## Overview

The E-Lingkod Dasol HRIS includes comprehensive privacy protection features designed to safeguard your personal information and comply with Philippine Data Privacy Act requirements. This guide explains how these protections work and what they mean for you.

### 🛡️ What's Protected

Your personal information is now protected by multiple layers of security:

#### Personal Employment Data
- Contact information and emergency contacts
- Employment history and position details
- Salary and compensation information
- Performance evaluations and ratings
- Leave balances and application history
- Document requests and certificates

#### Sensitive Information
- Government ID numbers and personal identifiers
- Medical information and health records
- Family and dependent information
- Educational background and certifications
- Training records and professional development

### 🔒 How Protection Works

#### Role-Based Access Control
The system automatically controls who can see what information:

**As an Employee, you can access:**
- ✅ Your own personal profile and employment data
- ✅ Your leave applications and balances
- ✅ Your document requests and certificates
- ✅ Your performance evaluations and goals

**As an Employee, you cannot access:**
- ❌ Other employees' personal information
- ❌ Colleagues' leave records or applications
- ❌ System-wide reports or analytics
- ❌ Administrative functions and controls

#### Automatic Data Filtering
The system automatically filters information to show only what you're authorized to see:
- **Navigation menus** show only functions you can use
- **Search results** include only your own data
- **Reports** are filtered to your authorized scope
- **API responses** contain only appropriate information

## 📋 Your Privacy Rights

### Right to Know (Transparency)

#### What Data We Collect
- **Personal Information**: Name, address, contact details
- **Employment Data**: Position, department, salary, performance
- **Identification Data**: Employee number, government IDs
- **Activity Data**: System usage, login times, access patterns

#### Why We Process Your Data
- **Employment Management**: Payroll, benefits, performance tracking
- **Legal Compliance**: Tax reporting, government requirements
- **Security**: Audit trails, access monitoring
- **Service Delivery**: HR services, document processing

#### How Long We Keep Data
- **Active Employment**: All data maintained for current employees
- **Former Employees**: Records retained per government requirements
- **Audit Logs**: Security logs kept for compliance periods
- **Backup Data**: Encrypted backups for disaster recovery

### Right to Access Your Data

#### What You Can Access
Through the system, you can view:
- Complete personal profile information
- All employment records and history
- Leave applications and approvals
- Document requests and certificates
- Performance evaluations and goals

#### Requesting Additional Data
For information not available in the system:
1. **Contact HR Department**: Submit written request with ID
2. **Specify Information**: Clearly describe what you want to access
3. **Receive Response**: Information provided within 15 working days
4. **Verify Accuracy**: Review provided information for completeness

### Right to Correct Information

#### Online Corrections
You can directly update:
- Contact information (address, phone, email)
- Emergency contact details
- Some personal preferences and settings
- Communication preferences

#### Requesting Complex Corrections
For changes requiring documentation:
1. **Submit Written Request**: Include supporting documents
2. **HR Review Process**: Verification of requested changes
3. **System Updates**: Changes made after approval
4. **Confirmation**: Notification when updates are complete

### Right to Object or Restrict Processing

#### When You Can Object
- Processing not required by law
- Marketing or promotional communications
- Automated decision-making systems
- Non-essential data collection

#### How to Object
1. **Contact Data Protection Officer**: Mr. Bryan Navalta Balaoing
2. **Specify Objection**: Clearly state what processing you object to
3. **Assessment Process**: Review of your objection and legal basis
4. **Response**: Decision communicated within reasonable timeframe

## 🚨 Privacy Violation Detection

### Automatic Protection Systems

#### Real-Time Monitoring
The system continuously monitors for privacy violations:
- **Unauthorized Access Attempts**: Blocked immediately
- **Unusual Activity Patterns**: Flagged for investigation
- **Data Export Attempts**: Logged and monitored
- **System Misuse**: Detected and reported

#### Violation Response Process
When a violation is detected:
1. **Immediate Blocking**: Unauthorized access prevented
2. **Alert Generation**: Security team notified instantly
3. **Investigation**: Detailed review of circumstances
4. **Corrective Action**: Measures taken to prevent recurrence

### Common Violation Scenarios

#### Employee Attempting Unauthorized Access
**What Happens:**
- Employee tries to view colleague's personal information
- System immediately blocks access
- Privacy violation alert generated
- User receives clear explanation message
- HR and security teams notified

**Your Protection:**
- Your data remains secure and private
- Violation attempt is logged for investigation
- No unauthorized access to your information
- Follow-up training for violating user

#### System Error Exposing Data
**What Happens:**
- Technical error might display wrong information
- Automatic error detection systems activate
- Exposed data immediately secured
- Affected users notified of potential exposure
- System fixes implemented rapidly

**Your Protection:**
- Immediate containment of any data exposure
- Investigation to determine scope of exposure
- Notification if your data was affected
- Enhanced monitoring for similar issues

## 📞 Reporting Privacy Concerns

### When to Report

#### Immediate Reporting Required
Contact support immediately if you:
- See other employees' personal information
- Notice unusual system behavior
- Receive error messages about privacy
- Suspect unauthorized access to your account

#### Examples of Privacy Issues
- Viewing colleague's leave balances or applications
- Seeing personal information in search results that shouldn't be there
- Receiving emails or notifications about other employees
- System displaying wrong personal information

### How to Report

#### Step 1: Document the Issue
- **Screenshot**: Capture what you see (if safe to do so)
- **Date/Time**: Note exactly when the issue occurred
- **Description**: Write detailed description of what happened
- **Context**: Explain what you were trying to do

#### Step 2: Contact Appropriate Support
**For Technical Issues:**
- Email: itsupport@dasol.gov.ph
- Phone: [Support Number]
- Include: Screenshots, error messages, detailed description

**For Privacy Concerns:**
- Contact: Mr. Bryan Navalta Balaoing (Data Protection Officer)
- Email: hrmo@dasol.gov.ph
- Include: Privacy concern details, potential impact

#### Step 3: Follow Up
- Acknowledge receipt of your report
- Investigation timeline and process
- Updates on resolution progress
- Confirmation when issue is resolved

## 🔍 Frequently Asked Questions

### General Privacy Questions

**Q: Why can't I see my colleague's contact information anymore?**
A: This is a privacy protection improvement. Employee contact information is now restricted to HR personnel and supervisors who need it for legitimate business purposes. If you need a colleague's contact information, you can ask them directly or contact HR for assistance.

**Q: How do I know if my personal information is secure?**
A: The system includes multiple security measures: all access is logged and monitored, data is encrypted in transmission and storage, and unauthorized access attempts are automatically blocked. You can also view your own access log to see when your information was accessed.

**Q: Can I see who has accessed my personal information?**
A: Yes, you can request an access log from the HR department that shows when your information was accessed, by whom, and for what purpose. This helps ensure transparency in how your data is used.

**Q: What should I do if I accidentally see someone else's information?**
A: Report it immediately to IT support or HR. Don't attempt to access the information again, don't share what you saw, and don't take screenshots. This helps protect the other person's privacy and allows the technical team to fix the issue.

### Rights and Procedures

**Q: How do I request a copy of all my personal data?**
A: Contact the HR department with a written request including your full name, employee number, and valid ID. They will provide your complete personnel file within 15 working days.

**Q: Can I request that certain information about me not be shared?**
A: In some cases, yes. Contact the Data Protection Officer (Mr. Bryan Navalta Balaoing) to discuss your specific concerns. Some data sharing is required by law (like tax reporting), but other sharing may be optional.

**Q: What happens if there's a privacy breach involving my data?**
A: You will be notified within 72 hours if a breach affects your personal information. The notification will explain what happened, what data was involved, what steps are being taken to fix the issue, and what you can do to protect yourself.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why does the system seem slower now?**
A: The enhanced privacy features include additional security checks that may slightly increase response times. These checks ensure your data is properly protected. If you experience significant slowness, contact IT support.

**Q: I'm getting error messages about not having permission. Is this normal?**
A: Yes, this is normal and indicates the privacy protections are working. The system now clearly explains when you don't have permission to access certain information or functions. If you believe you should have access, contact your supervisor or HR.

**Q: Can I access the system from home?**
A: If remote access is permitted by your department, yes. Always use a secure internet connection and follow all security guidelines. Never access the system from public computers or unsecured networks.
