## 📖 Featured Help Topics

### 🔐 Security Essentials

#### Password Security Best Practices
Strong passwords are your first line of defense. Learn how to create secure passwords that meet system requirements and protect your account from unauthorized access.

**Quick Tips:**
- Use at least 8 characters with complexity requirements
- Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Never share your password with anyone
- Change your password if you suspect it's compromised

[Read Full Guide →](security/password-management.md)

#### Understanding Privacy Protection
The system now includes enhanced privacy protection to safeguard your personal information and comply with Philippine Data Privacy Act requirements.

**Key Features:**
- Role-based access control limits who can see your data
- Comprehensive audit logging tracks all data access
- Automatic privacy violation detection and blocking
- Clear privacy rights and data subject request procedures

[Learn More →](security/privacy-protection.md)

### 📱 Using the System

#### Employee Self-Service Portal
Access and manage your personal HR information through the secure self-service portal designed specifically for your privacy and convenience.

**Available Services:**
- View and update personal information
- Apply for leave and track applications
- Request documents and certificates
- Access performance evaluation data

[Get Started →](employee/profile-management.md)

#### Leave Management Made Easy
The improved leave management system simplifies applying for leave while maintaining security and compliance with government regulations.

**New Features:**

- Automated approval workflows

- Instant status notifications

## 🎓 Training and Certification

### Required Training Modules

#### New User Orientation
Complete these modules before your first system login:
1. **Data Privacy Fundamentals** (Required for all users)
2. **System Security Basics** (Required for all users)
3. **Role-Specific Training** (Based on your job function)
4. **Emergency Procedures** (Required for all users)

#### Ongoing Education
Stay current with quarterly training updates:
- **Security Awareness Updates** (Every 3 months)
- **Policy and Procedure Changes** (As needed)
- **New Feature Training** (When system updates occur)
- **Compliance Refreshers** (Annual requirement)

### Certification Requirements
- **Initial Certification**: Complete all required modules with 70% or higher scores
- **Annual Recertification**: Complete refresher training and assessment
- **Specialized Certification**: Additional requirements for administrative roles
- **Continuing Education**: Minimum 4 hours annually for all users

### Training Resources
- **Online Learning Portal**: Self-paced modules available 24/7
- **Instructor-Led Sessions**: Scheduled group training sessions
- **One-on-One Support**: Individual assistance by appointment
- **Practice Environment**: Safe space to learn without affecting live data
