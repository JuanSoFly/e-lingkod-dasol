**Policy Owner**: Human Resource Management Officer (Data Protection Officer)

## Policy Statement

The Municipality of Dasol is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all employees in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (Republic Act No. 10173) and related regulations. This policy establishes the framework for lawful, fair, and transparent processing of personal data within the E-Lingkod Dasol Human Resource Information System and all related HR operations.

## Scope and Application

### Coverage
This policy applies to:
- All personnel with access to the E-Lingkod Dasol HRIS system
- All employees whose personal data is processed by the Municipality
- All contractors, vendors, and third parties handling employee personal data
- All personal data processing activities related to employment

### Geographic Scope
- This policy covers data processing within the territorial jurisdiction of the Philippines
- Cross-border data transfers are prohibited unless explicitly authorized under this policy

### Temporal Scope
- This policy is effective immediately upon approval
- Applies to all existing and future personal data processing activities
- Retroactive application to existing data stores and processing systems

## Data Protection Principles

### 1. Lawfulness and Transparency

#### Implementation Requirements
- All personal data processing must have a clear legal basis
- Data subjects must be informed of processing activities through privacy notices
- Processing purposes must be explicitly documented and communicated
- No hidden or undisclosed data processing activities

#### Operational Standards
- **Legal Basis Documentation**: Every processing activity must reference specific legal authorization
- **Privacy Notices**: Clear, accessible notices provided to all employees
- **Processing Records**: Comprehensive documentation of all data processing activities
- **Transparency Reports**: Regular reporting on data processing activities

### 2. Purpose Limitation

#### Core Requirements
- Personal data collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
- No further processing incompatible with original purpose without new legal basis
- Secondary use requires additional authorization and employee notification
- Purpose creep prevention through regular purpose audits

#### Practical Application
- **HR Administration**: Personnel management, payroll, and benefits administration
- **Legal Compliance**: Government reporting, tax obligations, and regulatory requirements
- **Performance Management**: IPCR, evaluations, and career development
- **Document Services**: Certificate generation and 201 file maintenance

### 3. Data Minimization

#### Minimization Standards
- Collect only personal data necessary for specified purposes
- Limit access to minimum necessary data for each user role
- Regular review and purging of unnecessary data
- Avoid collection of excessive or irrelevant information

#### Role-Based Implementation
- **Employees**: Access limited to own personal data only
- **HR Administrators**: Access to all employee data for legitimate HR purposes
- **Supervisors**: Limited access to direct report data for management purposes
- **IT Personnel**: Technical access only, no access to personal data content

### 4. Accuracy

#### Data Quality Standards
- Personal data must be accurate and kept up to date
- Reasonable steps taken to correct inaccurate data promptly
- Regular verification of data accuracy
- Employee self-service capabilities for data corrections

#### Correction Procedures
- **Employee-Initiated**: Self-service updates through HRIS portal
- **HR-Initiated**: Corrections based on official documentation
- **System-Generated**: Automatic validation and error detection
- **Third-Party**: Corrections from authoritative government sources

### 5. Storage Limitation

#### Retention Framework
- Personal data retained only as long as necessary for processing purposes
- Clear retention periods documented for each data category
- Automatic archival and deletion procedures implemented
- Legal and regulatory retention requirements respected

#### Retention Schedule
- **Active Employment Data**: Duration of employment
- **Post-Employment Records**: 5 years after separation (general rule)
- **Financial Records**: 10 years (BIR requirements)
- **Medical Information**: 10 years (liability protection)
- **Audit Logs**: 3 years minimum

### 6. Security (Integrity and Confidentiality)

#### Technical Safeguards
- **Access Controls**: Role-based permissions with multi-factor authentication
- **Encryption**: Data protection in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (database encryption)
- **Network Security**: Firewall protection and intrusion detection systems
- **Backup Systems**: Regular encrypted backups with secure storage
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive monitoring and activity tracking

#### Organizational Safeguards
- **Personnel Security**: Background checks and confidentiality agreements
- **Training Programs**: Regular data protection and security awareness training
- **Access Management**: Quarterly reviews and immediate revocation procedures
- **Incident Response**: Documented procedures for security breaches
- **Vendor Management**: Security requirements in all service contracts

## Data Subject Rights Implementation

### 1. Right of Access

#### Employee Rights
- Access to all personal data held by the Municipality
- Information about processing purposes, legal basis, and retention periods
- Details about data recipients and any international transfers
- Right to obtain copies of personal data in intelligible format

#### Implementation Process
- **Request Method**: Written request with valid identification
- **Response Time**: Maximum 15 working days
- **Format**: Digital copy provided unless otherwise requested
- **Verification**: Employee identity verification required before disclosure
- **Free Access**: First request per year provided free of charge

### 2. Right to Rectification

#### Correction Standards
- Prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- Notification to relevant third parties about corrections
- Documentation of all corrections for audit purposes
- No unreasonable restrictions on correction requests

#### Process Flow
1. **Request Submission**: Employee submits correction request with supporting documentation
2. **Verification**: HR verifies accuracy of correction request
3. **Implementation**: System updates made within 5 working days
4. **Notification**: Employee notified of completion
5. **Third-Party Updates**: Relevant agencies notified where applicable

### 3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

#### Limited Application in Employment Context
- Available when personal data no longer necessary for original purpose
- Employee consent withdrawn (where consent was legal basis)
- Data processed unlawfully or contrary to this policy
- Legal obligations require erasure

#### Restrictions and Limitations
- **Employment Records**: Core employment data retained during employment relationship
- **Legal Requirements**: Data required by law cannot be erased (tax records, etc.)
- **Legal Claims**: Data needed for legal defense retained
- **Public Interest**: Government function data retained as required

### 4. Right to Data Portability

#### Portability Standards
- Structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
- Employee-provided data and system-generated employment data included
- Secure transmission methods employed
- No adverse effect on others' rights and freedoms

#### Technical Implementation
- **Export Formats**: JSON, CSV, PDF options available
- **API Access**: Structured data export through secure API
- **Self-Service**: Employee portal for direct data export
- **HR-Assisted**: Complete data export through HR department

### 5. Right to Object

#### Objection Grounds
- Legitimate grounds relating to particular situation
- Objection to direct marketing (not applicable in employment context)
- Scientific or historical research purposes (with exemptions)
- Task carried out in public interest

#### Assessment Process
- **30-Day Response**: Municipality responds within 30 working days
- **Balancing Test**: Employee interests vs. legitimate organizational interests
- **Compelling Grounds**: Assessment of compelling legitimate grounds for continued processing
- **Alternative Measures**: Consideration of less intrusive processing methods

## Implementation Procedures

### Access Control Framework

#### User Authentication
- **Strong Passwords**: Minimum 8 characters with complexity requirements
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Required for administrators and sensitive data access
- **Session Management**: Automatic timeout and secure session handling
- **Account Monitoring**: Failed login attempt monitoring and account lockout

#### Authorization Management
- **Role-Based Access**: Three-tier system (Employee, HR Admin, Super Admin)
- **Principle of Least Privilege**: Minimum necessary access for job functions
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly access certification and annual comprehensive review
- **Immediate Revocation**: Access removed upon separation or role change

#### Permission Structure
```
Employee Role:
- employee.view-own (own profile only)
- employee.update-own (limited fields)
- leave.view-own (own leave records)
- leave.create (leave applications)
- document-approval.view-own (own requests)
- document-approval.create (request submissions)

HR Admin Role:
- All employee permissions PLUS:
- employee.view (all employees)
- employee.create/update/delete
- leave.view (all leave records)
- leave.approve/reject
- document-approval.view (all requests)
- document-approval.process
- reports.generate

Super Admin Role:
- All HR Admin permissions PLUS:
- user.manage
- system.configure
- audit.view
- backup.manage
```

### Data Handling Procedures

#### Data Collection
- **Necessity Test**: Collect only data necessary for specified purpose
- **Lawful Basis**: Document legal basis before collection
- **Notice Provision**: Provide privacy notice at point of collection
- **Consent Management**: Obtain and document consent where required

#### Data Processing
- **Purpose Alignment**: Process only for documented purposes
- **Access Logging**: Log all access to personal data
- **Quality Assurance**: Regular data accuracy verification
- **Security Measures**: Apply appropriate technical and organizational measures

#### Data Sharing
- **Internal Sharing**: Only with legitimate business need
- **External Sharing**: Only as required by law or with appropriate safeguards
- **Documentation**: Record all data sharing activities
- **Agreements**: Data processing agreements with all external recipients

### Incident Response Framework

#### Incident Categories
- **Privacy Breach**: Unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss of personal data
- **Security Incident**: System compromise or security vulnerability exploitation
- **Data Quality Issue**: Inaccurate or corrupted personal data
- **Access Violation**: Inappropriate access to personal data

#### Response Procedures

##### Immediate Response (Within 1 Hour)
1. **Contain Incident**: Prevent further damage or exposure
2. **Assess Scope**: Determine extent of incident and affected data
3. **Document Details**: Record all known facts about the incident
4. **Notify Management**: Alert HRMO (DPO) and IT management
5. **Preserve Evidence**: Secure logs and evidence for investigation

##### Investigation Phase (Within 24 Hours)
1. **Root Cause Analysis**: Determine cause and contributing factors
2. **Impact Assessment**: Evaluate potential harm to data subjects
3. **Risk Evaluation**: Assess ongoing risks and vulnerabilities
4. **Stakeholder Notification**: Determine notification requirements
5. **Remediation Planning**: Develop action plan for resolution

##### Notification Requirements (Within 72 Hours)
1. **Internal Reporting**: Report to Municipal Mayor and Legal Counsel
2. **Regulatory Notification**: Report to National Privacy Commission if required
3. **Data Subject Notification**: Notify affected employees if high risk
4. **Documentation**: Complete incident report with lessons learned
5. **Follow-up Actions**: Implement preventive measures and monitoring

## International and Cross-Border Considerations

### General Prohibition
- **Local Processing**: All employee personal data processed within Philippines
- **Storage Requirements**: All data stored on Philippine-based servers
- **Access Restrictions**: No remote access from foreign jurisdictions
- **Transfer Prohibition**: No transfer of personal data outside Philippines without explicit authorization

### Limited Exceptions
- **Legal Requirements**: Transfers required by Philippine law or court orders
- **Employee Consent**: Explicit, informed consent for specific transfers
- **Adequacy Decisions**: Transfers to countries with adequate protection
- **Appropriate Safeguards**: Transfers with binding corporate rules or standard contractual clauses

### Authorization Process
1. **Business Justification**: Clear business need for cross-border transfer
2. **Legal Review**: Legal assessment of transfer requirements
3. **Risk Assessment**: Evaluation of privacy risks in destination country
4. **Safeguard Implementation**: Appropriate protection measures in place
5. **DPO Approval**: Data Protection Officer authorization required
6. **Documentation**: Complete documentation of transfer basis and safeguards

## Policy Enforcement and Sanctions

### Violation Categories

#### Minor Violations
- **Examples**: Late completion of training, minor access control deviations
- **Sanctions**: Verbal warning, remedial training, written counseling
- **Documentation**: Incident report in personnel file
- **Escalation**: Pattern of minor violations escalated to major violation

#### Major Violations
- **Examples**: Unauthorized data access, privacy breach, policy non-compliance
- **Sanctions**: Written reprimand, suspension, demotion, termination
- **Documentation**: Formal disciplinary action in personnel record
- **Investigation**: Comprehensive investigation with due process

#### Criminal Violations
- **Examples**: Intentional data theft, malicious system access, data sale
- **Actions**: Immediate suspension, criminal referral, termination
- **Cooperation**: Full cooperation with law enforcement investigation
- **Civil Action**: Potential civil liability and damages

### Progressive Discipline Framework
1. **First Offense**: Counseling and additional training
2. **Second Offense**: Written warning and performance improvement plan
3. **Third Offense**: Suspension and final warning
4. **Fourth Offense**: Termination of employment
5. **Serious Offenses**: Immediate termination regardless of history
